## 🧭 About Me

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="62%" valign="top">

I am an **Ph.D. student in Civil Engineering (Transportation)** at the **University at Buffalo (SUNY)**, joining the **TRAVL Lab in Fall 2026**.

My research sits where **Computer Vision**, **Human Factors**, and **VR-based behavioral experiments** meet — building immersive testbeds and data-driven models that make streets safer for the people who walk them.

- 🔭 Modeling **pedestrian risk perception** from **biometric + computer-vision** signals
- 🥽 Designing **VR experiments** (Unity / Unreal Engine 5) to capture how people read autonomous vehicles
- 🧠 Exploring **Vision–Language Models (VLM)** for transportation scene understanding
- 🧪 Bridging **micro-simulation** (VISSIM, SUMO) with **behavioral data** from real & virtual streets
- 💬 Ask me about **Pedestrian–AV Interaction, VR Methods, Computer Vision, VLM**
- 📫 Reach me at **[wpark6@buffalo.edu](mailto:wpark6@buffalo.edu)**
